# v0.1.0-rc.1 Readiness

This v0.1.0-rc.1 readiness report is simulation-only. It does not claim hardware validation, foundry calibration, measured transfer matrices, production inference readiness, real hardware latency, real hardware energy efficiency, quantum advantage, hardware-native intelligence, or power-free computation.
